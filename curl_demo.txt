DEMO: Sentiment Classification API
==================================

1) Health endpoint
$ curl http://localhost:8000/health
{"status":"ok","model_loaded":true,"device":"cpu"}

2) Inference endpoint
$ curl -X POST "http://localhost:8000/predict" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"text":"The product is excellent and works perfectly."}'
{"text":"The product is excellent and works perfectly.","predicted_sentiment":"positive","positive_probability":0.9596,"negative_probability":0.0404,"threshold":0.5}

3) Second example
$ curl -X POST "http://localhost:8000/predict" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"text":"This was disappointing and stopped working after one day."}'
{"text":"This was disappointing and stopped working after one day.","predicted_sentiment":"negative","positive_probability":0.0764,"negative_probability":0.9236,"threshold":0.5}

These outputs were checked against the local FastAPI service and the saved model artifacts.
